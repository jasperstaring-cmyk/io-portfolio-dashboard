What it is

A live instrument for knowledge events

The IO Portfolio Dashboard is a second screen on stage. While the speaker shares their perspective, the screen shows a model portfolio responding to the conversation in real time.

Every topic a specialist brings — geopolitics, sustainability, rates, implementation, cost — lands on the same portfolio the audience manages every day. The dashboard makes that connection visible and immediate.

It is not an analysis tool. It is the connective thread of the programme — the constant element that translates each speaker's insight into the practice of the room.

Investment Officer deploys the Portfolio Dashboard at its knowledge events across the Benelux, Germany, France and the United Kingdom. Partners who speak at IO events have access to the full instrument for their session.

Compare mode

The moment of revelation

The operator reveals an alternative portfolio state live on stage. Bars shift, delta indicators appear. The contrast between the two states is legible from the back of the room.

Green = positive delta vs base.
Red = negative delta vs base.
The amber Compare label in the operator bar names the alternative scenario.
Explore mode

The audience takes the wheel

The most engaging moment of any session: the specialist asks the room a question, and the audience answers by moving the portfolio. Live sliders let anyone in the room shift allocations in real time — the chart responds immediately.

Green top line and 100% ✓ badge confirm the allocation is valid.
Sliders open automatically above the operator bar. The speaker can drive, or the operator responds to audience input.
Performance view

What the choice has historically meant

An optional time-series chart shows the historical return impact of the session's topic. Two lines — the current portfolio versus the alternative — make the consequence of the choice concrete.

Green line: portfolio as configured for this session.
Amber dashed: the alternative scenario. Only visible when Compare is active.
Key metrics panel on the right: YTD, 1Y, 3Y Ann., volatility, max drawdown.
The operator bar

Visible to the room — intentionally

The operator bar runs along the bottom of the screen and is always visible to the audience. That is a deliberate choice: it gives the instrument a live, professional character that reinforces the authenticity of the event.

How it works

Prepared before. Simple on the day.

The dashboard is configured ahead of the event — portfolio, policy questions, comparison scenarios. By the time the session starts, everything is in place. The instrument runs itself.

Before the event
Configure once

The portfolio is set up, each session gets a policy question and a chart dimension, and any comparison scenarios are defined. This is done via the built-in configurator — by whoever knows the programme best.

On the day
Navigate with arrow keys

Sessions are switched with the arrow keys. Compare is activated at the right moment. Explore opens when the conversation calls for it. No technical knowledge required on stage.

During the session
Speak. The screen follows.

The specialist focuses on the conversation. The dashboard visualises the argument in real time. In sessions with Explore, the audience can be invited to shift the portfolio themselves.

What the instrument provides

Everything set up. Nothing to worry about.

Your argument, made visible

Abstract insights become concrete the moment the portfolio changes on screen. The audience doesn't just hear the argument — they see it applied to the portfolio they manage every day.

A policy question that frames the session

A policy question is formulated for each session — in consultation with the speaker where relevant. It appears on screen throughout the talk, keeping the audience anchored to practice.

Audience interaction when it fits

Explore mode is optional. If the session calls for it, the audience can be invited to shift the portfolio live. If a linear presentation works better, that is equally supported.

No technical preparation required

The dashboard is configured before the event. On the day, everything is in place. A short briefing is enough to align on the one or two moments where a cue makes sense.
